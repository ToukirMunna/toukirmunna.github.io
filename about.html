My Approach

I'm Toukir Ahmed — a teacher by profession and an Android developer by passion. I got into building apps the same way most hobbyists do: I couldn't find something that worked exactly the way I wanted, so I built it myself.

I don't follow a product roadmap or chase market trends. Every app I've made started with a genuine personal need — whether that was tracking daily expenses, helping kids learn on a safe platform, or keeping a private diary. That keeps the work honest and the motivation real.

I believe well-crafted software doesn't need to come from a big studio. It needs to be thoughtful, fast, and respectful of the person using it.
