Our Engineering Manifesto

Four uncompromising design principles guiding every application built at Toukir Studio.

100% Offline-First

Every app functions entirely without internet connectivity. Your local databases (Room/SQLite) reside strictly on your device with zero mandatory server sync.

Zero Ads & Telemetry

No advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, no third-party analytic bloat. Pure utilities crafted with deep respect for your privacy, bandwidth, and attention.

AMOLED & Battery Native

Engineered with true-black (#000000) AMOLED dark modes and minimal CPU overhead to eliminate background battery drain and extend device longevity.

Modern Jetpack & Compose

Built in native Kotlin leveraging modern Android architecture components, coroutines, and declarative Jetpack Compose UI for fluid 120Hz performance.

Behind Toukir Studio

Crafted by an Independent Developer & Educator

Toukir Studio is an independent Android software lab founded by Toukir Ahmed — an educator and native Android software craftsperson based in Bangladesh.

Frustrated by modern apps bloated with subscriptions, predatory telemetry, and intrusive popups, Toukir builds focused native Android utilities. Today, the catalog spans 31+ specialized tools — ranging from distraction-free spiritual companions and offline dictionaries to everyday utilities — built for people who value speed, privacy, and craftsmanship.
