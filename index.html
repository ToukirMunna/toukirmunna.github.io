Engineering Values

App Guidelines

Principles guiding every application engineered at Toukir Studio.

01

Bloat-free by design

Every tool has a single, well-defined purpose. No unnecessary features, no subscription upsells, no popups begging for ratings, and zero gimmicks.

02

Small app size footprint

Applications are built with lean native Kotlin and minimal dependencies, keeping download sizes small and memory consumption light on any device.

03

No telemetry, no tracking

Zero analytic SDKs, zero user tracking, and zero advertising frameworks. The software does not monitor what you do or phone home with your behavioral data.

04

Offline first always

Full functionality without an active internet connection. Databases reside strictly on your device flash memory, with optional personal Google Drive backups you control.

05

No selling of data

Your information is never collected, monetized, or shared with third parties. Software is provided cleanly and transparently as open releases.
